## khan-explainer (design reference)
https://github.com/haiderfarooq3/khan-explainer, MIT License.
Fablecast re-implements, in TypeScript and without copying code, these ideas from it:
the voice drives the clock; `p` runs 0..1 while a beat is spoken and `seg(p, from, to)` orders strokes;
the primitive names `write`, `stroke`, `arrow`, `check`, `line`, `curve`, `oval`, `box`, `ring`, `around`, `seg`;
the board flipping between chalk-on-black and marker-on-white; the WARN lint loop that fails tests.
Fablecast is not affiliated with Khan Academy.
